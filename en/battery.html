Do I need a new battery?

iPhone: Settings → Battery → Battery Health & Charging. If Maximum Capacity is below 80%, a new battery is well worth it.

Android: the setting differs by brand. If you can't find it, just bring the phone in and we'll test it.

It's not only the number: if the phone shuts down suddenly even at 85% health, the battery may still be the cause.

Swollen battery? Please be careful

  1. Stop chargingCharging a swollen battery is dangerous.
  2. Don't press it back inSqueezing a battery can start a fire.
  3. Bring it in soonA swollen battery can also push and break the screen. The sooner, the cheaper.

FAQ

How long does it take?

About 30 minutes for most models, and you can wait in the shop. Some batteries need to be ordered; we'll let you know when they arrive.

Will I lose my data?

No. Replacing the battery doesn't touch your data. Your phone will be exactly as before.

At what battery health should I replace it?

We generally recommend it below 80%. If the phone still feels fine to you, there's no rush. If it shuts down suddenly, come in for a check even if the number looks OK.

Can I just walk in?

Yes. If you send us your model on LINE first, we can check the battery is in stock so you don't have to wait.
