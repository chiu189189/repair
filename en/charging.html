What we check first

  1. Lint or dust in the portPocket lint often blocks the port — a cleaning alone fixes many phones.
  2. A different cable and chargerTo rule out a cable problem.
  3. Charging currentTo tell whether the port is broken or the charging circuit on the logic board.

Common repairs

Charging port flex cable: on iPhones the port shares one flex cable with the microphone and antenna lines, so the whole cable is replaced.

Charging sub-board: many Android phones have the port on a small board, which we replace.

Logic board charging circuit: if a new port doesn't fix it, the charging chip on the board may be faulty — we can do chip-level repair.

FAQ

Wireless charging works but cable charging doesn't. What's broken?

Usually the charging port or lint inside it; the logic board is normally fine. A quick check in the shop will confirm.

Can I clean the port with a toothpick?

Don't use metal or anything sharp — it can scratch the contacts. A wooden toothpick used gently is OK, but if you're unsure, let us do it.

How long? Will I lose data?

Most port replacements take about 1 hour and don't touch your data.

It says “Liquid Detected” and won't charge.

Unplug the cable and let the phone dry for a while. If it keeps appearing, the port may have moisture or corrosion — bring it in for a check.
