    About the prices
    • Prices show only the range (e.g. NT$ 3,•••). The exact price depends on part costs and your phone’s condition — we quote after checking it in the shop or on LINE.
    • Water damage, drops and self-repair attempts are not covered by the warranty.
    • Logic board, no-power and water damage problems are quoted after testing. Chip-level repair is our speciality — if another shop said it can't be fixed, ask us.
    Which screen should I choose?
    • Same-spec: picture and touch close to the original. Higher price.
    • Aftermarket: budget-friendly; colour and brightness slightly below the original. Good for a tight budget or a spare phone.
    • After a screen replacement on iPhone 11 and later, or a battery on XS and later, Settings shows a “non-genuine part” message. This is normal and doesn’t affect use.
