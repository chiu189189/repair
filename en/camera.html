Common repairs

Lens glass only: if just the outer glass is broken and the camera works, we replace only the glass — the cheapest option.

Rear / front camera replacement: for a black screen, shaking focus or dots inside, the camera module usually needs replacing.

Logic board check: if a new camera still shows black, the circuit on the board may be faulty — we can do chip-level repair.

After an iPhone camera replacement, Settings may show an “Unknown Part” message. We will tell you in advance.

FAQ

Only the lens glass is cracked. Do I need a new camera?

No. If photos are only a bit hazy, replacing the glass is usually enough. We check the camera itself in the shop first.

The camera shakes and makes a clicking sound. Why?

Usually the stabiliser or focus part inside the camera is broken — common after a drop or vibration from a motorbike phone mount. The camera module needs replacing.

How long does it take?

About 1 hour for most models. If a part needs ordering, we'll arrange a time with you.
