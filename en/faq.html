Before you come

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Just walk in. If you want to be sure the part is in stock, send us your model and the problem on LINE first. We're open Tuesday to Sunday, 12:00–22:00, closed Mondays.

Is there parking?

Yes, you can park right in front of the shop. The address is No. 189, Yuandong St., Longjing Dist., Taichung 434 — Tunghai shopping area, near Tunghai University.

What should I prepare?

① Back up your data (iCloud, Google or a computer). ② Decide whether to leave your passcode — we use it only to test the phone after repair; if you prefer not to, we can test it together when you pick it up. ③ Please keep your SIM card, memory card, case and screen protector yourself.

Should I turn off Find My iPhone?

Some repairs need it turned off for a while, for example testing after a logic board part is replaced. We'll tell you when you drop off the phone.

Which phones do you repair?

iPhone and iPad, plus Android phones and tablets from Samsung, OPPO, vivo, Xiaomi, Google, ASUS and more. Not sure about your model? Ask us on LINE.

I live outside Taichung. Can I mail my phone in?

Yes. Before sending, tell us your model and the problem on LINE. Once we confirm we can fix it and explain how to ship it, send it to the shop (臺中市龍井區東海里遠東街189號). We test and quote first, repair only with your OK, then ship it back.

Testing and prices

Do you charge for testing?

General testing is free. Phones that won't turn on, water damage and logic board problems need to be opened and measured — if you decide not to repair after testing, there's an inspection fee of NT$300.

Are the website prices final?

They're reference prices. The final price is the quote we confirm with you in person after testing. It's written on the repair ticket, and we start only after you agree.

If you find another problem during repair, will you just charge more?

No. If we find another fault, or the cost would go over the quote, we always contact you first and continue only with your OK. If you say no, we put the phone back to how it was (where possible) and return it.

Do I pay if it can't be fixed?

No repair charge if we can't fix it. For phones that won't turn on, water damage and logic board problems, the NT$300 inspection fee applies.

Why aren't all prices shown on the website?

Part prices change often, and the same problem can have different part options. Send us your model and the problem on LINE and we'll give you the latest, most accurate price.

Parts

Same-spec vs aftermarket parts — what's the difference?

Same-spec parts look and feel close to the original and cost more; aftermarket parts are cheaper with slightly weaker colour and brightness. We explain the difference and price before repair, and you choose. We never use non-original parts without telling you.

After the repair my iPhone shows “Unknown Part”. Is that normal?

Yes. After a screen replacement on iPhone 11 and later, a battery on XS and later, or a camera replacement, Settings may show this message. It's how Apple's system works and doesn't affect normal use. Some features (such as True Tone or battery health) may be affected — we'll explain before quoting.

Can I keep my old parts?

Yes, just tell us before the repair.

Is my phone still water-resistant after the repair?

Once a phone has been opened, we can't guarantee factory-level water resistance. Please try to keep it away from water.

Data and privacy

Will the repair erase my data?

Most repairs (screen, battery, charging port, camera) don't touch your data. Logic board, water damage and dead phones can still lose data during repair, so please back up if you can.

Will you look at my photos or messages?

No. We never view, copy, back up or send out your photos, messages, contacts or accounts. After repair we only test that the phone works (touch, camera, calls, charging and so on).

Do I have to give you my passcode?

No. A passcode lets us test the phone after repair and is used only for that. If you'd rather not, we can test it together when you pick it up.

Will my phone be sent somewhere else?

No. All repairs are done in our shop, and for most of them you can watch.

Time and pickup

How long does it take?

Battery about 30 minutes, screen and charging port about 1 hour — you can wait in the shop. Water damage about 1–3 days; logic board problems depend on testing. See each repair page for details.

Will you tell me when it's ready?

Yes, by LINE or phone. If you add our LINE official account and link your repair ticket, you'll get a message when it's done and can check the progress.

What do I bring for pickup?

Show your repair ticket or lookup code, and test the phone with us before taking it home.

Can I pick it up later?

Yes. If it's not picked up within 30 days after we notify you, we'll contact you again.

About Apple

Are you an Apple Authorized Service Provider?

No, we're an independent repair shop. Apple products repaired by us may no longer be covered by Apple's warranty.

My phone is still under Apple warranty or AppleCare+. Should I come to you?

Check with Apple first — it may cost less. Come to us if Apple's quote is too high, the warranty has ended, or you want to keep your data on a logic board repair.

Do you buy used phones?

Yes. Please bring your ID in person and sign out of your Apple ID / Google account first. See our trade-in page (Chinese).

Message us on LINE or call — just tell us your model and the problem.
