About us

Our shop, equipment, parts policy and business details.

Who we are

CHIU189 is a walk-in phone repair shop in the Tunghai area of Longjing, Taichung. Besides everyday screen and battery replacements, we specialise in phones that won't turn on or got wet — problems that need work on the logic board.

What we fix, how long it takes and how to book: see the home page. Prices by model: see the price list.

Skills and equipment

The owner trained professionally in chip-level repair and worked in other repair centres before opening this shop in Taiwan. For dead or water-damaged phones we first find the part that's actually broken, and repair that part instead of replacing the whole board. More on chip-level logic board repair.

  • Stereo microscope, micro-soldering and reballing tools for chip work and jumper wires
  • DC power supply and charging port tester to locate faults by current
  • Board schematics and boardview software to trace the original circuits
  • Anti-static workbench and specialist tools

Parts and transparency

When quoting, we tell you which part we'll use (for example a same-spec or aftermarket screen) and the price difference, and you choose. After replacing a screen, battery or other part on an iPhone, Settings may show a “non-genuine part” message — we'll tell you in advance.
