Screen options

Same-spec screen: closest to the original in colour, brightness and touch. Higher price.

Aftermarket screen: budget-friendly and fine for daily use; colour and brightness are slightly different from the original.

Glass-only repair: on some models, if only the outer glass is broken and the display works, we can replace just the glass. Booking required, takes 3–4 hours, and carries some risk — if it fails, the whole screen has to be replaced.

We explain the options and price differences before any work, and you decide. After an iPhone screen replacement, Settings may show an “Unknown Part” message. Your phone works normally, and we will tell you in advance.

FAQ

Will I lose my data?

Normally no. We only replace the screen and don't touch the logic board. If the phone was also damaged inside by the drop, there is still some risk, so please back up first.

Same-spec vs aftermarket — what's the difference?

Mainly picture quality (colour, brightness, response) and price. Same-spec feels closest to the original; aftermarket is cheaper. You can compare them in the shop.

Only the glass is broken. Can you replace just the glass?

On some models, yes — as long as the display underneath works and isn't leaking. Glass-only repair needs a booking, takes 3–4 hours and carries some risk. We'll explain before quoting.

How long does it take?

About 1 hour for most models. You can wait in the shop or walk around the Tunghai shopping area. If a part needs to be ordered, we'll arrange a time with you.
