Phone got wet? Turn it off and bring it in fast

The real danger isn't the moment it gets wet — it's the corrosion that slowly eats the logic board afterwards. Even if it still turns on, it should be opened and cleaned soon.

Sound familiar?

  • Dropped in waterToilet, drain, washing machine, pool, sea
  • Rain or spilled drinksDrinks and coffee corrode faster than clean water
  • Acting strangeWater marks on screen, quiet speaker, foggy camera
  • Won't turn onOr keeps restarting

What to do right away

  1. Turn it off nowPower speeds up corrosion, and a short circuit can burn parts.
  2. Don't charge it or keep testing it“Let's see if it still turns on” is the most common way phones get worse.
  3. Dry the outside, remove the SIM and caseSo water isn't trapped inside.
  4. No hair dryer, no riceHeat damages parts, and rice can't reach water inside the phone — it only wastes time.
  5. Bring it in as soon as possibleThe sooner it's opened and cleaned, the better the chance of saving it.

FAQ

It still turns on after getting wet. Is it OK?

Not necessarily. Impurities in the water slowly corrode the board, and problems often appear days or weeks later. Cleaning it early costs less than repairing it after it fails.

My phone is waterproof, so it's fine, right?

Water resistance is tested on new, perfectly sealed phones. Age, drops and earlier repairs weaken the seals, and hot water, sea water and drinks aren't covered anyway.

Can my data be saved?

It depends on how badly the board is damaged. Many wet phones turn on again after cleaning and chip-level repair, and then the data is still there. We need to test it first.

How long does it take?

Cleaning, drying and testing take about 1–3 days. If the board needs repair, we'll tell you the time after testing.
