What is “chip-level repair”?

A phone's logic board has thousands of tiny parts — chips, capacitors, resistors — and traces thinner than a hair. A dead phone is often just one broken part or one broken trace.

Many shops can only swap the whole board or say it can't be fixed. Chip-level repair means finding that one faulty part under a microscope, replacing only it, and bringing the whole board back to life.

What we repair

  • Chips and capacitorsFind shorted or burnt parts and replace them
  • Jumper wiresReconnect broken traces or lifted pads with ultra-fine copper wire
  • CPU reballingWhen solder under the CPU cracks after a drop, we remove, reball and re-solder it
  • Charging and power circuitsWhen it won't charge or power on even with good cables, we trace the circuit on the board

How we find the fault

  1. Measure the currentWith a DC power supply we see where the start-up stops.
  2. Check the schematicsBoard schematics and boardview show the parts on that circuit.
  3. Measure under the microscopePart by part until we find the faulty one.
  4. Quote — we only repair with your OKWe tell you what's broken, how we'll fix it and what it costs.
  5. Repair and full test before pickupPower, charging, signal, camera and more are all tested.

Will my data still be there?

Chip-level repair fixes your original board instead of replacing it, so your data is usually still there after the repair. That's why many customers choose it — they don't want to lose their photos and chats.

Board damage varies, so we can't guarantee every phone can be fixed. Dead, water-damaged and logic board phones must be opened and measured; if you decide not to repair after testing, there's an inspection fee of NT$300. If we can't fix it, there's no repair charge.

FAQ

Another shop said it needs a new board or can't be fixed. Can you repair it?

Often “can't be fixed” just means that shop doesn't do chip-level repair. Bring it in — after testing we'll tell you clearly whether it can be fixed and roughly how much it costs.

How long does a board repair take?

It depends on the fault. Simple ones are done the same day; complex ones take a few days. We'll tell you after testing.

Can you fix Face ID?

Face ID parts are paired to the logic board, so it can't always be repaired — it depends on which part is broken. We'll tell you after testing.

Do you repair phones other than iPhone?

Yes. We also repair logic boards of Samsung, OPPO, vivo, Xiaomi, Google, ASUS and other Android phones.
